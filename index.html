Driver information

Frequently asked questions.

Answers to common questions drivers ask before applying.

Are all miles paid, including loaded and empty miles?

Yes. The advertised rate is $0.75 CPM flat and applies to all miles, including loaded and empty miles.

Is there a mileage band?

No. The advertised compensation uses a flat $0.75 CPM rate.

Can I drive solo or as a team?

Yes. The current offer is available for solo or team drivers, subject to qualification and equipment availability.

How often is pay deposited?

Direct deposit is made weekly on Friday.

Is there a sign-on bonus?

A $5,000 sign-on bonus is advertised, subject to eligibility and applicable company terms.

Is there a driver referral bonus?

Yes. The current advertised referral bonus is $500, subject to the applicable referral program terms.

Are detention and layover paid?

Yes. Detention and layover pay are offered according to the company's current compensation policy.

Are extra stops paid?

Yes. Extra stops are paid separately according to the current compensation policy.

What trucks do you use?

The fleet includes Volvo and Freightliner Cascadia trucks, with model years ranging from 2020 through 2026.

Are the trucks automatic?

Yes. The listed fleet uses automatic transmissions.

What type of trailer do you use?

The current position uses 53-foot dry van trailers.

What are the main lanes?

The current lanes include the South, Southeast, and Southwest.

What is the governed truck speed?

The trucks are governed at approximately 69–70 MPH.

What are the home-time options?

The current schedule is 2 weeks out / 2 days off, 3 weeks out / 3 days off, or 4 weeks out / 4 days off. Each additional week out adds one additional day off.

What is the minimum time out?

The minimum scheduled time out is 2 weeks. Certain long-haul states require a minimum of 4 weeks out.

Which states require 4 weeks out?

The listed long-haul states are New York, Florida, Arizona, Utah, Colorado, Wyoming, and North Dakota.

Are pets allowed?

Yes. Pets are allowed according to current company policy.

Where do drivers fuel?

Love's fuel cards are provided.

Are tolls covered?

Toll passes are provided according to the company's current policy.

Is there an on-site repair shop?

Yes. An on-site repair shop is available.

Is dispatch forced?

Dispatch is not forced. Drivers are encouraged to work with dispatch to help identify available freight and mileage opportunities.

Are there driver-facing cameras?

The company does not use inward-facing cameras.

What ELD system do you use?

The company uses Samsara ELD tablets.

Can I take my 34-hour reset at home?

Yes, when your home is reasonably close to your route. OTR drivers may take resets on the road depending on their schedule.

Are cash advances available?

Yes. Cash advances are available according to current company policy.

What are the CDL requirements?

The current requirements include at least 2 years of CDL-A experience, a clean Clearinghouse and drug-test qualification, and no SAP, DUI, or DWI according to the listed hiring requirements.

How many violations are allowed?

The listed requirement allows a maximum of 3 minor violations within the last 3 years.

How many preventable accidents are allowed?

The listed requirement allows a maximum of 1 preventable accident within the last 3 years.

Is truck abandonment allowed?

No. The listed requirements specify no truck abandonment history.

How much travel assistance is provided?

Travel assistance is available up to $250, with airport pickup available according to the orientation arrangements.

Where is orientation?

Orientation is held at 565 Anderson Dr, Unit B, Romeoville, IL 60446.

How long is orientation?

Orientation is approximately 24 hours.

Is drug testing available before orientation?

Drug testing may be completed on-site or prior to orientation, depending on the individual case. The listed orientation process requires the drug test before noon.

How quickly can I start?

The current recruiting offer advertises a potential start within 24 hours for qualified drivers, subject to completing the required hiring and qualification process.
